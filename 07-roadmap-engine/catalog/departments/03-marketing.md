# 03 — Marketing (MKT)

In most Belgian SMEs marketing is a hat, not a department: the owner or an office
employee posts "when there's time", an external agency does the website. The leak is
under-execution (things simply don't happen), not inefficiency. AI shifts the
constraint from writing skill to judgment and consistency. Consultant-side authoring
source; adapt per client.

## MKT.010 — Marketing planning & calendar
- **Typical AS-IS:** no plan, or a January good-intentions list; activity is reactive (a quiet week triggers a post).
- **Value leaks:** `no-visibility` (nobody knows what was done or what worked).
- **Opportunity patterns:** quarterly calendar co-drafted from seasonality + sales priorities (agent-T1 sparring); recurring content slots as fixed automation triggers. Caveat: a calendar without an owner is decoration — assign the marketing hat first (F4 role card).

## MKT.020 — Content creation (posts, blogs, cases)
- **Typical AS-IS:** writing takes evenings; drafts stall in perfectionism; the agency invoice per text feels too high, so nothing ships.
- **Value leaks:** `skill-bottleneck` (writing skill/confidence), `wait` (content queued behind daily work).
- **Opportunity patterns:** draft-first writing from job photos/voice notes in a fixed brand voice (agent-T1 — the highest-adoption marketing win); repurposing one case into post/mail/website variants (agent-T1, expansion). Caveat: generic AI tone is a real risk — build a voice guide from the owner's best texts before scaling output.

## MKT.030 — Social media management
- **Typical AS-IS:** sporadic posts on LinkedIn/Facebook/Instagram; comments and DMs checked irregularly; no scheduling.
- **Value leaks:** `skill-bottleneck` (one person can post), `no-visibility` (no view on reach or leads generated).
- **Opportunity patterns:** batch-create + schedule a month of posts (hybrid: agent drafts, human curates, scheduler automates); comment/DM triage into the lead flow (agent-T2). Caveat: outbound replies to the public stay T1 until tone is proven.

## MKT.040 — Email campaigns & newsletters
- **Typical AS-IS:** a newsletter "we should really send more often"; customer list exported from accounting, cleaned by hand each time.
- **Value leaks:** `retype` (list wrangling per send), `skill-bottleneck` (writing + tool knowledge in one person).
- **Opportunity patterns:** synced audience list from the customer base (automation); newsletter drafted from the month's jobs/news (agent-T1). Caveat: GDPR consent basis for the list must be verified before any automation scales the sending.

## MKT.050 — Website & SEO upkeep
- **Typical AS-IS:** website built once by an agency, then frozen; texts outdated; every change is a paid ticket with weeks of lead time.
- **Value leaks:** `skill-bottleneck` (vendor-locked edits), `wait` (agency queue).
- **Opportunity patterns:** content drafting for service pages/FAQs in-house (agent-T1), agency only for structure; SEO gap analysis vs. local competitors (agent-T1 research). Caveat: check what the CMS allows the client to edit themselves before promising velocity.

## MKT.060 — Review & reputation management
- **Typical AS-IS:** Google reviews arrive unmanaged; asking happy customers for reviews is forgotten; negative reviews sting and sit unanswered.
- **Value leaks:** `chase` (manual review-asking never happens), `no-visibility` (rating drifts unobserved).
- **Opportunity patterns:** post-job review-request automation (automation); review-response drafting in brand tone (agent-T1). Caveat: responses to negative reviews always get human review — one tone-deaf AI reply costs more than the automation saves.

## MKT.070 — Campaign performance reporting
- **Typical AS-IS:** "does our marketing work?" answered with anecdotes; ad-platform stats never connected to quotes won.
- **Value leaks:** `retype` (screenshots into slides, if reported at all), `no-visibility` (spend not linked to revenue).
- **Opportunity patterns:** monthly channel snapshot auto-pulled into one page (automation); plain-language interpretation and next-step suggestions (agent-T1). Caveat: attribution in an SME is directional at best — report trends, not false precision.

## MKT.080 — Events & trade shows
- **Typical AS-IS:** yearly fair prepared in a rush; leads collected on paper or badge scans that nobody processes afterwards.
- **Value leaks:** `chase` (post-event follow-up evaporates), `wait` (leads contacted weeks later, cold).
- **Opportunity patterns:** photo-of-notes/badge → structured lead + personalised follow-up draft within 48h (agent-T1 feeding SAL.010); checklist automation for event logistics. Caveat: the win is speed of follow-up — measure time-to-first-contact, not mails sent.
