# 09 — HR & people (HR)

Below ~50 FTE, HR is the office manager plus the external social secretariat
(payroll provider). The scarce resource is candidates and retention, not process
elegance — but the admin friction is real and lands on people who have three other
jobs. Monitoring-adjacent tooling is change-sensitive: involve the team early
(F4 §6). Consultant-side authoring source; adapt per client.

## HR.010 — Recruitment & job postings
- **Typical AS-IS:** vacancy text recycled from 2019; posted on VDAB/Indeed and the website; employer branding nonexistent; hard profiles (technicians!) get zero response.
- **Value leaks:** `wait` (vacancies open for months cap growth), `skill-bottleneck` (nobody writes compelling postings).
- **Opportunity patterns:** posting variants per channel in an honest company voice (agent-T1, draft-first); auto-posting and applicant-intake structuring (automation). Caveat: AI won't fix an uncompetitive offer — if response is zero, the diagnosis is market position, not copy.

## HR.020 — Candidate screening & interviews
- **Typical AS-IS:** CVs judged by gut between two other tasks; slow responses lose scarce candidates to faster employers; interview quality varies by interviewer mood.
- **Value leaks:** `wait` (days-to-first-response loses candidates), `skill-bottleneck` (structured interviewing skill absent).
- **Opportunity patterns:** CV summary against criteria + suggested interview questions per candidate (agent-T1); instant acknowledgment and scheduling automation (automation). Caveat: AI-assisted selection touches GDPR and the EU AI Act's high-risk employment category — AI prepares, humans decide, and the decision trail must show it.

## HR.030 — Employee onboarding
- **Typical AS-IS:** first day improvised: no badge, no login (see IT.010), no plan; contracts and Dimona handled by the social secretariat under time pressure.
- **Value leaks:** `chase` (equipment, access and documents chased across departments), `error` (missed legal steps — Dimona, work-permit checks).
- **Opportunity patterns:** onboarding checklist workflow with owners and deadlines (automation); personalised welcome/instruction pack per role (agent-T1). Caveat: cheap to fix, high symbolic value — a good first day is retention work; position it that way, not as admin efficiency.

## HR.040 — Time & absence registration
- **Typical AS-IS:** paper timesheets or a punch clock feeding Excel; leave requested by mail/WhatsApp and tracked in a shared sheet; disputes at payroll time.
- **Value leaks:** `retype` (sheets consolidated by hand), `error` (wrong balances, forgotten overtime), `chase` (reminders to submit hours).
- **Opportunity patterns:** app-based registration with balance self-service (automation); anomaly digest for the manager (agent-T2). Caveat: registration tooling can trigger works-council information duties and CAO constraints — check before rollout, not after (F4 §6).

## HR.050 — Payroll preparation for the payroll provider
- **Typical AS-IS:** monthly variables (overtime, premiums, absences, meal vouchers) assembled from sheets and mails, then keyed into the social secretariat's portal; errors surface as wrong pay.
- **Value leaks:** `retype` (double entry into the portal), `error` (wrong pay → trust damage and corrections).
- **Opportunity patterns:** automated variables export from time registration to the portal (automation); pre-submission plausibility check vs. last month (agent-T2). Caveat: check the social secretariat's own import/API options first — often an activation, not a build.

## HR.060 — Training & certification tracking
- **Typical AS-IS:** VCA, BA4/BA5, driver qualifications tracked in Excel or discovered expired during a site audit; Belgian individual training rights (5 days) unadministered.
- **Value leaks:** `no-visibility` (expiries surprise), `chase` (certificates hunted per audit).
- **Opportunity patterns:** certification register with expiry alerts and booking prompts (automation); training-plan drafting per role from the skills matrix (agent-T1). Caveat: compliance angle (site access, insurance) makes this urgent beyond convenience — an expired VCA can idle a whole crew.

## HR.070 — HR admin & personnel files
- **Typical AS-IS:** contracts, addenda, fleet documents, attestations spread over binders, mailboxes and the payroll portal; every employee question means searching.
- **Value leaks:** `retype` (same data in five places), `wait` (simple questions take days).
- **Opportunity patterns:** single digital personnel file (automation/tooling); HR-FAQ agent over policies for common questions — leave rules, expenses, car policy (agent-T2). Caveat: personnel data is GDPR-sensitive — access control and data location questions come before any agent reads a single file.

## HR.080 — Performance & feedback cycle
- **Typical AS-IS:** annual reviews skipped in busy years; no written objectives; feedback happens only when something breaks.
- **Value leaks:** `chase` (HR chases managers to hold reviews), `skill-bottleneck` (managers avoid difficult conversations).
- **Opportunity patterns:** cycle reminders + preparation templates (automation); conversation-prep sparring for managers, including the difficult ones (agent-T1). Caveat: an agent must never write the judgment — it prepares the human's own observations; anything else is felt as insulting and probably is.
