## Part A — Business basics (Q1–7)

- HVAC & sanitary installation company, Aalst (Oost-Vlaanderen), founded 1998 by the current owner's father; owner-managed since 2015.
- ±35 FTE: 4 binnendienst (sales/quotes/onthaal), 18 techniekers, 3 planning, 4 admin/finance, rest management & aankoop.
- Customers: residential renovation, small B2B (retail units, small offices), ±60/40 particulier/professioneel [VERIFY — split estimated by owner, not measured].
- Revenue ±€7.5M, stable-to-slightly-growing [VERIFY — 2025 annual accounts not yet filed at time of intake].
- Growth ambition: not more volume with more people — "hetzelfde volume met minder gedoe, en de dienst na verkoop laten groeien" (recurring maintenance revenue).
- Region: ±45 km around Aalst; no plans to expand geographically.

## Part B — Current operations & pain (Q8–12)

- Core flow: aanvraag → (werfbezoek) → offerte in Excel → uitvoering → papieren werkbon → facturatie in Exact.
- Loudest pains, in the owner's order: (1) quote lead time ("wij verliezen werken omdat onze offerte er vijf dagen over doet"), (2) paper werkbonnen and evening paperwork for techniekers, (3) invoicing weeks behind the work, (4) maintenance contracts tracked in an old Excel.
- Quote volume estimated ±45/month; werkbonnen ±85/week; verkoopfacturen ±190/month (owner's estimates, to re-check per department).
- Planning is praised: one experienced planner holds it together — also named as a single-person risk.
- Handover sales → planning happens by forwarding mails; things get lost ("vraag dat aan de planning, die kunnen daarover meespreken").

## Part C — Systems & data (Q13–16)

- Office 365 (Outlook, Excel, Teams), Exact Online for bookkeeping (external accountant on quarterly rhythm), bank app with card reader for payments, WhatsApp groups for the field.
- No CRM, no field-service tool, no planning software (physical board + shared Outlook calendar). Werkbonnen on paper (drieluik).
- Loved: Exact + Peppol flow since Jan 2026 ("dat heeft de accountant goed opgezet"). Hated: the Excel quote template ("prijzen kloppen nooit").
- Knowledge lives in mailboxes, server folders per client, and heads. Data trust: low for hours and materials, medium for financials.
- No existing automations beyond bank/Peppol.

## Part D — AI today (Q17–20)

- Official use: none. Shadow use: owner uses a free ChatGPT account for lastige mails; one binnendienst medewerker uses it for offerteteksten "af en toe" [VERIFY — depth of shadow use, pulse survey in Phase 1].
- Past attempts: none. Sentiment: curious-pragmatic at the top; techniekers expected to be sceptical of anything that smells like controle/monitoring.
- Red lines stated by owner: no customer data into free tools; techniekers must not get "een tweede gsm-administratie" pushed on them; his own approval of big quotes stays.

## Part E — People & change (Q21–25)

- Workforce: technicians long-tenured (avg >8 years), low turnover; binnendienst younger.
- Opinion leaders: the planner (Rita) and one senior technician — "als die twee mee zijn, volgt de rest".
- Past change history: switch to Exact in 2019 went fine; a 2021 attempt at a planning app died ("te veel klikken, na drie weken deed niemand het nog") — key lesson for adoption design.
- No works council, no union delegation. One family atmosphere, one WhatsApp group.
- Appetite: owner wants visible wins within a quarter; explicitly warned against "een systeem kopen en dan trekken".

## Part F — Engagement mechanics (Q26–28)

- Sponsor & decision maker: zaakvoerder (Dirk). Project lead / day-to-day contact: office-verantwoordelijke (binnendienst 1).
- Budget: comfortable with a fixed diagnostic package; implementation budget decided per business case, payback <6 months expected for wave 1.
- Timeline: discovery workshops in June, AS-IS walkthroughs before the builders' holiday (bouwverlof), roadmap workshop in July. Hard constraint: no pilot go-lives in the sept–dec maintenance peak for the field team.
- Confidentiality: standard NDA signed 2026-05-28; no financial details to staff beyond what they already know.
