The master context document for this engagement — the single source of truth
that personalizes every other asset. Generated from intake notes via
**Prompt 0.1** (`02-phase-playbooks/phase-0-intake-discovery.md`); the sponsor
validates the facts before Phase 1 starts.

**Tag convention**
- `[INTAKE]` — still to be filled from the intake interview
  (`00-system/client-intake-questionnaire.md` → `intake/intake.md`).
- `[VERIFY]` — captured but uncertain; the client must confirm before it is
  treated as fact. Never invent numbers; anything unverified keeps its tag.

Rules: only information from actual notes; under 3 pages; English regardless of
the notes' language, but verbatims stay in the original language.
