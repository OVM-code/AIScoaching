# Econocom — Tailored Engagement Plan

How the generic 7-phase flow adapts to a multi-BU, multi-country, works-council, listed-company client. Read together with `client-context.md` (same folder) and `00-system/engagement-flow.md`.

## Scope strategy: group ambition, contained wave 1

Do **not** attempt group-wide transformation in one engagement. Recommended shape:

1. **P0–P2 at group level** (sponsor: group exec) → group AI strategy-on-a-page aligned to One Econocom, portfolio across BUs.
2. **P3–P5 in ONE lighthouse BU/country** (pick at G2 using criteria: leadership pull, data readiness from P1, contained P&L, works-council climate). Belgium is often a good lighthouse for Econocom-type groups: mid-size, HQ-visible, bilingual assets reusable for FR. [Decide with sponsor]
3. **P6 designs the group scale mechanism**: hub-and-spoke CoE (F4 §5 default fits Econocom's matrix), playbook for BU-by-BU rollout — wave 2+ runs BU by BU with the client's own CoE leading and you coaching.

## Phase adaptations

| Phase | Adaptation for Econocom |
|---|---|
| P0 | Stakeholder map must cover: group exec committee, 3 BU heads, 2-3 country MDs, group IT/CIO, group HR, works-council landscape. Expect competing agendas between BUs — the sponsor question ("where does this sit?") is existential. |
| P1 | 10–15 interviews stratified per BU + lighthouse country deep-dive. Run the maturity scan **per BU** — the variance IS the finding. Add an extra assessment lens: *AI in the client offering* (what Econocom sells) vs *AI in operations* — leadership will conflate them; the readout must separate them. |
| P2 | Strategy must ladder into One Econocom explicitly (cross-BU synergy = data/process integration = AI leverage). Portfolio balanced: group-level use cases (bid/tender factory, cross-BU CPQ) + BU quick wins. Business cases in the lighthouse BU's real numbers. Likely wave-1 candidates to test against P1 evidence: RFP/tender analysis & response drafting · service-desk ticket triage + knowledge agent · lease-contract data extraction & end-of-lease processing · cross-BU quote assembly. |
| P3 | Org redesign only for the lighthouse BU + the new group AI capability (hub). The organigram deliverable therefore has two parts: lighthouse BU target org (F4 full treatment) and group hub-and-spoke design (F4 §5). Works-council choreography per country is on the critical path — engage group HR/legal in week 5, not week 8. |
| P4 | Pilots inside the lighthouse. Tool selection must respect group IT standards & security (get CIO office into D21 scoring). Expect procurement latency — start vendor checks 3 weeks earlier than the generic plan. |
| P5 | Two-tier change: group narrative (exec-level, investor-safe language — listed company: nothing in internal comms you wouldn't defend externally) + lighthouse-local ADKAR execution. Champions network seeded per country early, even outside the lighthouse, to prep wave 2. |
| P6 | The CoE stand-up (D32) is the real product at this scale: hub roles, funding model (group-funded year 1, BU-charged later), the reusable asset library (prompts, eval sets, agent specs from pilots), and the BU rollout playbook. Compliance (D31) at group level once, inherited by BUs. |

## Timeline & effort (indicative — calibrate at intake)

~20–24 weeks to G4 in the lighthouse; your effort concentrated in P0–P3 (2–3 days/wk) tapering to coaching cadence in P4–P6 (1 day/wk). Gates as in the master flow, plus a **BU-heads alignment checkpoint** between G2 and G3 (the political gate — the org redesign lands only if BU heads co-own it).

## Econocom-specific risks

| Risk | Move |
|---|---|
| BU rivalry turns the portfolio into horse-trading | Score in a cross-BU workshop with F3's explicit criteria; sponsor pre-commits to honoring scores |
| "We sell digital, we know this" antibodies | Frame as capture-what-we-sell: the assessment compares internal maturity to what they promise clients — the gap is the urgency story |
| Matrix accountability diffusion | Every use case gets ONE owner in ONE P&L; RACI-A discipline from D17 |
| Listed-company communication risk | All broad comms via the change-story review; workforce numbers never in writing before D18 sequencing |
| Acquisition churn reshuffles sponsors | Decisions log in client-context.md kept current; re-confirm sponsorship at every gate |
