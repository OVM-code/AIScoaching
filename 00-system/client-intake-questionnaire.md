# Client Intake Questionnaire

Run this as a 60–90 min interview with the sponsor (+ a written pre-send of Part A so numbers are ready). The answers feed **Prompt 0.1**, which turns them into `client-context.md` — the single source of truth that personalizes every other asset.

For SMEs, use the ⭐-marked questions only (~30 min).

## Part A — Business basics (send ahead)

1. ⭐ Legal name, locations, website, languages used internally.
2. ⭐ What does the business do, for whom? Main services/products and customer segments.
3. ⭐ Headcount (total + by function: field/operations, sales, admin/back office, management). Org chart if it exists.
4. ⭐ Revenue range and rough margin picture (ranges are fine). Growth ambition for the next 3 years.
5. Business units / departments and what each does. Who leads each?
6. ⭐ Top 3 strategic priorities for the next 12–24 months (in their words).
7. Seasonality, regulatory constraints, certifications, union/works-council presence.

## Part B — Current operations & pain

8. ⭐ Walk me through your core process end-to-end (e.g., lead → quote → job → invoice → payment). Where does it hurt?
9. ⭐ Which 3 activities eat the most hours relative to the value they create?
10. ⭐ Where do errors, rework, or customer complaints concentrate?
11. What work exists only because information has to be retyped / looked up / chased?
12. ⭐ What can't you do today that you wish you could (capacity, speed, offering)?

## Part C — Systems & data

13. ⭐ List your software: ERP/accounting, CRM, planning/dispatch, communication, HR, sector tools. What's loved, what's hated?
14. ⭐ Where does critical knowledge live — systems, spreadsheets, or people's heads?
15. Data quality: could you trust a report on jobs/margins per customer generated from your systems today?
16. Any integration/automation already in place (Zapier/Make/Power Automate, RPA, scripts)?

## Part D — AI today

17. ⭐ Who already uses AI tools (officially or privately)? For what? What's the vibe — excitement, fear, indifference?
18. Any past AI/automation attempts? What happened?
19. ⭐ What triggered this engagement now? What does success look like in 12 months, concretely?
20. Any red lines (things AI must never do here)?

## Part E — People & change

21. ⭐ Age/tenure/digital-savviness profile of the workforce, in broad strokes.
22. Who are the informal opinion leaders? Who resisted the last big change, and why?
23. How did the last major change (new system, reorg) actually go?
24. ⭐ Appetite for org changes: is restructuring roles/teams on the table, or only tooling?
25. Works council / union constraints on process or role changes?

## Part F — Engagement mechanics

26. ⭐ Sponsor, day-to-day lead, decision process, budget envelope (range).
27. ⭐ Timeline expectations and any hard deadlines (board meeting, season start…).
28. Confidentiality constraints on what I may process with AI tooling.

---

## Interview technique notes

- Ask for **stories, not opinions**: "tell me about the last time a quote went out late" beats "are quotes slow?".
- Quantify on the spot: "how many per week? how long does one take? who does it?" — these numbers power every business case later.
- Note verbatim quotes (with role, not name) — gold for the change story in Phase 5.
- Close by asking: "If we do only ONE thing in 90 days, what should it be?" — calibrates expectations against the eventual prioritization.
