# M3 — 90-Day Go-To-Market Plan

Objective: **an audience seed + 1–3 paying Kickstart clients (ICP-A) and 1 corporate conversation in motion (ICP-B) within 90 days.** Implements LEADS-3/4/5/6, BIZ-3.

## Operating rhythm (non-negotiables, weekly)

- **10 outreach touches/working day** (warm first; cold from week 4) — LEADS-4 calibrated for high-touch B2B
- **3 posts + 20 meaningful comments/week** on LinkedIn (+ crosspost where the niche lives — see watering holes)
- **Friday scorecard** (15 min, template below)
- Everything you build for a client (anonymized) becomes content (BIZ-5)

## Phase B — Audience & pipeline (week 2–8)

**Warm outreach (primary, LEADS-5):** 10/day through the 100-list with A-C-A scripts (`assets/outreach-scripts.md`). The ask is never "buy" — it's the free AI-Scan debrief or "who do you know that runs an installatiebedrijf?". Track referral chains — in the trades, the second-degree contact is the buyer.

**Content (compounding):** 3×/week per M4 (build-log, use-case-with-numbers, myth-busting). 20 comments/week on posts where owners hang out (sector federations, ERP/planning-tool vendor pages, accountants). Comments on others' posts outperform own posts for the first 500 followers.

**Ecosystem (the trades are offline-first):**
- Join/attend: Embuild (construction federation) local events, Bouwunie, Techlink (installation sector), Unizo/Voka network evenings, sector fairs (e.g., Install Day, Batibouw pro days) [VERIFY calendar]
- Offer every federation/local chapter a **free 45-min talk**: "10 uur per week terugwinnen met AI — wat werkt écht in een installatiebedrijf" (= the lead magnet as a talk; collect scans live)
- **Partner channel** (one conversation/week): accountants & boekhoudkantoren (they see the admin pain and are trusted advisors), planning/field-service software resellers, sector consultants (safety/quality advisors). Deal: you make them look good with their clients; referral fee or co-branded scan optional.

**Cold outreach (from week 4):** 5/day to mapped local businesses (Google Maps + review count = proxy for size), NL 3-touch sequence in the scripts file. Personalize line 1 from their website/reviews — an agent you build for yourself can draft these (eat your own dog food; that build is also a content post).

## Phase C — First client & flywheel (week 4–13, overlaps B)

1. Scan debriefs → discovery calls per `assets/discovery-call-script.md` (LEADS-6 targets below).
2. **First 3 clients at founder pricing** traded — explicitly, in the agreement — for: measured before/after numbers, a video testimonial, a written case story, 2 introductions (BIZ-3, OFFER-4).
3. Deliver the Kickstart loudly: weekly build-log posts (with client consent), the client tells the story at their federation chapter if willing.
4. At S5 (results session): retainer offer + the referral ask same day — satisfaction peaks at the results readout.

## The corporate track (ICP-B, parallel, low volume)

- Week 1–2: list 10 warm routes into Econocom-type organizations (ex-colleagues, LinkedIn 1st degree, your network's CFO/CIO contacts). The Econocom materials in `03-clients/econocom/` are your proof-of-method.
- Ask (A-C-A): not "hire me" but *"I've built a full methodology for AI integration incl. org redesign and change management — 30 minutes of brutal feedback from someone who lives this at scale?"* Feedback calls convert to pilots without ever pitching (Enns POS-3).
- Publish 1 corporate-grade artifact/month (EN or FR): e.g., "How AI changes the organigram — a working method" from F4. This is authority content for ICP-B while the weekly NL content serves ICP-A.
- Target: 5 such conversations by day 90; 1 progressing toward a paid diagnostic (Phase 0–1 of the full system, sold per BIZ-2/POS-3 — diagnosis first, never free strategy).

## Metrics & Friday scorecard (LEADS-6)

| Metric | Weekly target | Funnel math to 1 client/mo |
|---|---|---|
| Outreach touches | 50 | 200/mo |
| Conversations started | 10 | 40/mo |
| Scans delivered / debriefs booked | 4 | ~15/mo |
| Discovery calls | 1–2 | 5/mo |
| Offers made | — | 2–3/mo |
| Kickstarts closed | — | 1/mo |

Diagnose weakest ratio, not vibes: no conversations → volume/scripts (LEADS-4/5); debriefs but no calls → lead magnet's reveal is weak (LEADS-2); calls but no closes → offer/guarantee (OFFER-1/3) or call script.

**Day-90 review:** if <2 clients — check volume first (it's volume 80% of the time), then run M1 §F's diagnostic order. If ≥2 clients + retainers starting: raise price [SET+20%], drop founder terms, start the referral engine (every S5 = 2 asks) and consider the rung-2 paid workshop as the new top-of-funnel.
