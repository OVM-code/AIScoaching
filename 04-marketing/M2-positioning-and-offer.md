# M2 — Positioning & The Offer

Implements: POS-1/2/3, OFFER-1/2/3/4/5, BIZ-1/2 (see M1).

## 1. Who we serve (two motions, two ICPs)

### ICP-A — the public motion (content, outreach, lead magnet)
**Flemish home-services / installation KMO**: HVAC, sanitair, elektro, dakwerken, renovatie, cleaning, tuinaanleg.
- 8–50 medewerkers, owner-led (often family), €1–10M revenue
- Symptoms: owner/office drowning in paperwork; quotes go out days late; **can't find technicians** (the sector's #1 constraint); evenings eaten by admin; knowledge in the owner's head
- Buying psychology: allergic to hype and consultants-with-slides; trusts peers, numbers, and people who speak vakman-taal; fears wasting money and looking foolish
- Competitive alternatives (POS-1): do nothing / hire another office admin (€45–55k loaded, if findable) / send someone to a generic AI workshop / nephew-with-ChatGPT

### ICP-B — the corporate motion (network, authority, referral — NOT public funnel)
Belgian/French mid-to-large enterprises in digital-adjacent services (Econocom archetype): multi-BU, works councils, transformation agendas. Reached via warm introductions, the published methodology (the repo/system as proof), speaking slots, and the SME case studies as evidence of hands-on capability. Sold as the full 7-phase engagement. **The public message stays niched on ICP-A** (POS-2) — corporates respect a specialist with proof more than a generalist with a broad site.

## 2. Positioning statement (Dunford format)

> For **owners of installation- and service businesses (8–50 people)** who **lose hours and growth to admin and can't hire their way out**, I am the **AI-integration coach for de vakman-sector** who **rebuilds how the work flows — people, automations and AI agents together — starting with working automations in weeks, not reports**. Unlike **generic AI consultants and one-day workshops**, I **work in your business, with your people, on your real jobs, with a guaranteed outcome — and I leave you able to run it without me**.

One-liner (for bios, MSG-2): *"Ik help installatie- en servicebedrijven 10+ uur per week terugwinnen met AI en automatisering — zonder extra aanwervingen."*

## 4. Guarantee (OFFER-3) — pick one, put it in writing

1. **Conditional outcome (recommended):** "Als de meting na 90 dagen geen 10 uur per week aan vrijgekomen capaciteit aantoont, werk ik gratis verder tot het er staat." Conditions: sessions attended, agreed data provided, pilot actually used (behaviors you need anyway).
2. **Milestone refund:** "Geen werkende automatisering na sessie 4? Volledige terugbetaling." (Stronger close, riskier scoping — use once delivery is proven.)
3. **Anti-guarantee** for the corporate track: no guarantees, diagnosis-first, references instead (Enns posture; corporates distrust guarantees).
